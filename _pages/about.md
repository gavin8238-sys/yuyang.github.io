I am a Master's student in **Environmental Science and Management** at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://hkust.edu.hk/), starting from August 2025. I received my Bachelor's degree in Environmental Science from [Hunan Agricultural University](https://www.hunau.edu.cn/) in June 2024, where I ranked **1st out of 42** students with a GPA of 89.67/100.

Research Interests
======
My research focuses on **membrane technology**, **water treatment**, and **new energy battery recycling**. I am currently working on integrating heterogeneous multi-source battery data into a unified knowledge graph, and exploring synergistic processes coupling seawater desalination with spent lithium battery recycling.
